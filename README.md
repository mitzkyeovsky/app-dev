# Arcane
Arcane is an animated series set in the world of League of Legends. It mainly focuses on the growing tension between two cities — **Piltover**, known for its progress and innovation, and **Zaun**, a place filled with chaos and struggle. The story revolves around characters whose lives are deeply affected by the conflict between these two cities.


 ## Genre
-	Action-adventure
- Adult animation
- Science fantasy
- Steampunk

## Why I liked Arcane
What I loved most about Arcane is its art style — the way it’s animated with such beautiful graphics and stunning visuals is truly astonishing. The music also adds a strong emotional impact, making every scene feel alive and powerful. For me, this is one of the greatest shows that has ever captured my full attention.

Arcane currently has two seasons, and I’m still eagerly waiting for the next one because of how interesting and unpredictable the story is. Every episode leaves me wanting more, and I honestly can’t get over how each ending makes me think about what will happen next.

Beyond its visuals and storytelling, the series also offers meaningful lessons. One that stood out to me is that innovation and progress don’t always lead to good outcomes — sometimes, too much progress can cause destruction and imbalance. 

> “In pursuit of great, we failed to do good.” - *Viktor*

This quote reminds me that progress should always come with limits. Sometimes, in our desire to achieve something great, we forget the simple good things that matter most. This message really stayed with me, and it’s something I want to share with others who also enjoy the show.
